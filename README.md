# 🌸 GlowVeda – Cosmetic Recall Notification Tool

GlowVeda is a product safety platform designed to help customers, retailers, and QA teams stay informed about cosmetic product recalls. It simplifies recall notifications, batch verification, and communication across the supply chain.

## 🚨 Problem
Customers often continue using harmful or recalled cosmetic products due to lack of timely alerts. Retailers & QA teams struggle to track recall actions efficiently.

## Prototype: https://glowveda262.netlify.app/

## 💡 Solution
GlowVeda enables:
- Real-time recall alerts  
- Batch/lot number verification  
- Role-based dashboards  
- Automated notifications to customers  
- Central recall tracking for QA teams
